Press Scan market to find items you can buy and flip to a city vendor.

Prices come straight from the Torn API and move fast — always confirm the listing in-game before buying. “Prof. qty” and “Total profit” only look at the cheapest 20 listings of each item. The vendor shown is the one the Torn API lists for the item; if it lists none, the cell shows “—”. Bazaar prices aren’t available through Torn’s API, so only the Item Market is scanned. Rows grey out when their data is older than the “stale” setting — use ↻ or Verify & open to re-check one item before you act on it.
